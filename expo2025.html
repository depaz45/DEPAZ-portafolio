EXPO 2025

Project

Door To Door

Role

Project Manager & Designer

Tools

Figma | Adobe Illustrator | Canva

Door To Door is a website created to connect Salvadorans living abroad with shipping agencies and independent delivery services, making it easier to access products from El Salvador. The platform was designed to provide a reliable way to compare agencies and find the best delivery options. I contributed to project planning, branding, logo creation, website design, and the final pitch presentation.
