MechNow! is a prototype mobile application designed to connect drivers with mechanics and repair shops in emergency situations. The project focused on creating a practical technological solution for users who experience vehicle problems while on the road. I worked on the interface design and prototype development using Figma, helping create a practical and user-friendly solution.
